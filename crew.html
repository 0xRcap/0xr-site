The record

I cofounded Boveda in 2022. We sold decentralized storage on the Filecoin network and won 1.4 petabytes of client data, which then had to physically live somewhere: machines in a Madrid data centre.

Next was Fungi, a ChatGPT for crypto that executed onchain actions in plain English. The go-to-market was a token, and it reached $9M market cap on Solana with around a thousand people in the Telegram. When we pivoted to a yield agent I launched it on a 24 hour livestream instead of an announcement, building it while people watched. It had $50k in assets under management in the first week.

After Fungi became Sail my work was ecosystem growth. The agent plugged into 56 yield sources, and each one meant a team to launch with, co-marketing that ran on both accounts. It moved $700M autonomously and beat the stablecoin yield market, and the products I cofounded passed $1.1M in assets under management. Sail is open source now, a permission layer other agents can transact under.

We never paid a PR agency, a design studio or an influencer. I raised more than $1M as a cofounder and ran every surface the story travelled on, brand, film, comms and product, alone at first and then with six agents whose work I edit all day into one shared creative vision. Four million impressions since 2024 came out of that crew. Running it on my own companies first is the only reason I can sell it to yours.
