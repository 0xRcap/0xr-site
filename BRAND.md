# INNIE BRAND — v1 "ink"

*The studio's identity. Companion to [INNIE.md](./INNIE.md) (the constitution) and
[SOUL.md](./SOUL.md) (the characters). This governs how the STUDIO looks and speaks;
each innie has its own soul and, later, its own universe bible. Amendments are
commits, never vibes.*

---

## 1. What innie is, worn on the outside (one breath)

A boutique studio that gives brands living characters. It speaks rarely and in ink:
black and white, printed texture, hand-drawn warmth. Everything it publishes looks
like it came off a press, not out of a feed.

## 2. Asset zero — the reference image

The founding image: two children mid-duel with wooden swords, laughing, rendered in
high-contrast manga screentone on white. It is the brand thesis in one panel:

- **Wooden swords.** Bokken are practice weapons — the tools of craft, not combat.
  We rehearse in public. Mastery through play.
- **Laughter mid-duel.** The duel is joyful. Rivalry as delight, not menace. The
  studio's seriousness never reads as solemnity.
- **Children.** Characters that are unmistakably *alive* — wanting, moving, mid-act.
  That's the bar for every innie: alive, not posed. A mascot poses; a character acts
  (INNIE.md §3).
- **Screentone on white.** Halftone dots, ink blacks, paper white — the texture of
  something printed and handmade. Warmth achieved inside strict monochrome.

The image lives at `assets/reference/00-wooden-swords.*` (file to be dropped in by
Rodrigo; described here so all design work can proceed against it). Every future
visual is tested against it: same energy, same ink, same joy.

## 3. The name

**innie — the inner one.** Every brand has an outside: the logo, the campaigns, the
quarterly voice. An innie is the inside: the character who actually lives in the
universe, remembers everything, wants something, and does the work of being known.
Always lowercase. The word is ours to define; we never explain it twice.

## 4. Visual law

1. **Ink on paper.** Paper-warm white grounds, sumi black, halftone gray between.
   No gradients, no glow, no neon, no photography as identity.
2. **One color exists: seal red.** A single vermillion — used *only* for the seal
   (§5). If red appears anywhere else, it's a mistake. The restraint is what makes
   the stamp land.
3. **Screentone is the texture signature.** Halftone dots and hatching wherever a
   lesser brand would use a gradient. The tell that something is ours at thumbnail
   size.
4. **Figurative, always.** People, characters, hands, motion — never abstract
   geometry as identity. We sell characters; the brand is drawn like one.
5. **Manga grammar.** Panel energy: motion lines, caught mid-moment, white space
   doing the work. Every image implies the frame before and after it — a character
   is a story, not a pose.
6. **The one-second test** (inherited from HEAT's bible): recognizable without a
   logo in the first second, or it doesn't ship.

## 5. The seal

The studio's sigil is a **seal** — a carved stamp in seal red, hanko-style, pressed
onto everything the studio releases. Two laws:

- **Every innie is born with its own seal.** Carving the seal is the last act of a
  universe build — the character's signature mark, stamped on all its work.
- **The seal IS the disclosure.** Stamped means synthetic, and proud of it. This is
  INNIE.md §3 ("never undisclosed") made beautiful instead of apologetic — the
  disclosure law turned into the brand's most collectible element.

## 6. Voice of the studio

The studio speaks rarely; the innies speak often. When the studio itself speaks:

- **Letters, not posts.** Long-form, considered, occasional. The studio account is
  a workshop journal, not a feed.
- **Quiet, precise, warm.** Craft vocabulary: carve, press, ink, rehearse, stamp.
  Confidence without volume — no announcements in all caps, no thread-boy cadence.
- Words we own: **innie, soul, universe, canon, the seal, the studio, character,
  drop, rehearsal.**
- Words we never use: **AI-powered** (never market the ingredient), **content**
  (we make films, posts, drops, characters), **engagement/viral** (we say a thing
  *traveled*), **synergy/leverage** (agency-speak), **mascot** (the thing we are not).

## 7. Reference point: Nous Research — what we take, what we don't

Nous proved that a serious technical operation can wear a mysterious black-and-white
art identity and have the culture do its marketing for it — the aesthetic *is* the
moat, and drops treated as artifacts compound into mystique.

**We take:** the monochrome discipline; drops as artifacts, not announcements;
community-first gravity (people repost the art before they understand the product);
never explaining the mystique.

**We leave:** the esoteric coldness. Nous is ciphers and gnosis; innie is warm and
figurative — laughing kids with wooden swords, not sigils in the void. Their mystery
says *we know something you don't*; ours says *there's someone alive in here*.

## 8. Relation to HEAT

The engine wears ember; the studio wears ink. HEAT's artifacts (the map, ignition
films, the ledger) keep HEAT's brand — the ember ramp, terminal DNA, the isotherm
sigil. innie's own surfaces are monochrome + seal. When HEAT's instrument texture
appears inside studio work, it appears as what it is: the machine glimpsed through
the workshop door, never redecorated.

## 9. What never ships

- Anything that looks generated-by-default: purple gradients, glassmorphism, stock
  3D blobs, corporate-memphis people.
- The machine explained (scoreboard public, machine private — inherited law).
- An undisclosed innie. The seal goes on everything or it doesn't go out.
- Solemnity. If a piece has no play in it, it isn't ours yet.
