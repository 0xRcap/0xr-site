01

Allé Studios

Sep 2026 – now

Brand, film, comms and product. Running now.

A Madrid streetwear label built on four pillars, music, art, cinema and sport, one era per collection, with the founder at the centre and never the face. The positioning came first: every claim the brand makes was audited against eleven competing labels at their listed prices, the eras frame was named as the axis none of them run, and it settled into one sentence everything else inherits.

The current capsule is MID 90'S, 1990s New York, and the campaign for it started before the engagement did: an AI film set inside the era, made for the label and run on its own account, and a MID 90'S mark drawn in the same liquid chrome as the label's own monogram, so the capsule carries the house style rather than a borrowed one.

Running every month: four collection character films, six UGC videos on two AI faces run against each other, twenty photographs with their captions and posting order, and a signature film with its cutdowns at the drop. Around the slate, the go-to-market: the channel plan and posting calendar, one activation, collab options, and the product page.

The MID 90'S reel, made before the engagement, ran on the label's own account: 83,200 views and 367 likes.

02

Sail

Oct 2025 – now

Brand, film, comms and product.

Rebranded Fungi into Sail and took the yield agent institutional. It was the first agent to run swaps and bridges autonomously and without custody, and it outperformed the stablecoin yield market.

The AI films started here, and verified followers and users climbed once the films began shipping.

Sail is now a protocol and a harness: the first open source permission layer agents can transact safely under, with Sailor on top of it.

$700M in autonomous volume and $1M AUM at Sail, organic, across roughly 1,000 Sail users and 56 yield sources integrated.

03

Fungi

Dec 2023 – Oct 2025

Brand, film, comms and product.

Started as a natural language interface for DeFi, a ChatGPT for crypto that executed onchain actions in plain English. The token launched as go-to-market reached $9M market cap on Solana, with a Telegram community of around 1,000.

Pivoted to the yield agent and launched it with a 24-hour livestream instead of an announcement, hitting $50k AUM in the first week. I ran every surface it travelled on: brand, film, comms and product.
