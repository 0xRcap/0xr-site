What media is now.

Medium is Latin for the middle: the thing that carries a message from whoever made it to whoever receives it. Media is the plural.

  1. 01

    Media is everything
    that carries your story to a person.

    Your brand, your films, your words and the product itself. All four carry it, so all four are media.

  2. 02

    Where the idea came from

    until 2005

    Old media

    Your story travelled on channels somebody else owned. You pitched a journalist and waited.

    2005

    Every company a media company

    Tom Foremski named it in 2005. Companies stopped pitching and started publishing on channels they owned.

  3. 03

    Why every company, and why now

    Whatever you make, somebody else can make it too. The tools are rented by the month, the factory takes anyone’s order, and your competitor is calling the same model you are.

    When was the last time you tried something completely different from the rest? You can probably name one. And soon enough somebody copies it, then somebody copies the copy, and the thing that felt different is on every menu.

    Everything you sell is becoming a commodity.
    The story is the only part you still own.

    And a story does not live on one page of a website. It arrives through the brand, the films, the words and the product in someone’s hands. That is the new part: all four surfaces, run as one operation. Feeding them used to take a room full of people, which is why only the funded few ever did it.
