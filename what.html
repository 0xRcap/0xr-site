What new media is.

Medium is Latin for the middle. A medium is the thing in between, and its job is to carry a message from whoever made it to whoever receives it.

  1. 01

    So what is media?

    Media is everything
    that carries your story to a person.

  2. 02

    Where does media live?

    Media lives on every surface
    that carries your story to a person.

    Brand What stays the same on every other surface. brand identity / logotype / typography / art direction
    Film The story with time in it. campaign films / AI video / character systems / product films
    Comms The story in words, on a schedule. copywriting / content systems / social / community
    Product The surface they touch. product design (UI/UX) / web design / design systems

  3. 03

    Where new media came from

    until 2005

    Old media

    Your story travelled on channels somebody else owned. You pitched a journalist and waited.

    2005

    Every company a media company

    Tom Foremski named it in 2005. Companies stopped pitching and started publishing on channels they owned.

    2025

    New media, as a department

    a16z made it a department and proved it works. It took a room full of people, so only the funded few ever got one.

  4. 04

    What I believe

    AI collapsed the production half of that department. It left the half where someone decides what is worth making, which is the half you are paying for.

    Every company will run a media department.
    Most will run it with one (or few) people.

    The judgment stays human. The production goes to the agents.
